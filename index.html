About Us

IronCore Fitness opened its doors in September 2026 with one simple goal — to make professional training affordable for everyone. Our floor is divided into dedicated cardio, strength and functional zones so that members never have to wait for their favourite machine.

Every new member gets a free orientation with one of our certified trainers, who helps plan a routine based on fitness level and goals. Whether you want to lose weight, build strength or simply stay active, there is a programme for you.

"We wanted a gym that feels welcoming on day one — no judgment, no pressure, just honest training and results." — Akshut Goyal, Founder
